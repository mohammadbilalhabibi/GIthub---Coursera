Simple Interest Calculator

This project explains how to calculate simple interest.

Formula

Simple Interest = (Principal × Rate × Time) / 100

Where:

Principal (P): The initial amount of money.
Rate (R): The annual interest rate in percentage.
Time (T): The time period in years.
Example

If the principal is 1000, the annual interest rate is 5%, and the time period is 2 years:

Simple Interest = (1000 × 5 × 2) / 100 = 100

The simple interest is 100.
